Ridgeline

Privacy Policy — Effective May 2026

The short version

Ridgeline collects nothing. No accounts, no analytics, no tracking. All your progress stays on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. Ridgeline does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on external servers.

All game data — your settings, run history, mission progress, and trophy case — is saved only in your device’s local storage using the standard iOS preferences system. That data never leaves your device.

Game Center

Ridgeline integrates with Apple Game Center to support optional leaderboards and achievements. If you choose to connect Game Center, Apple’s own privacy policy governs that data. BoomPowApps only submits scores and achievement status to Game Center; we do not receive or store any Game Center account information.

Game Center is entirely optional. The game works in full without it.

Third-party services

Ridgeline uses no third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, crash reporting services, or tracking frameworks of any kind.

No analytics No ads No crash reporting No tracking No accounts required

Data stored on your device

The following is stored locally using iOS standard storage and is never transmitted to BoomPowApps:

Game settings Best run distance Total distance run Avoid chain records Mission progress & tier Trophy case Tutorial status

You can clear this data at any time by deleting the app.

Children’s privacy

Ridgeline is rated 4+ and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new effective date. We will not make material changes without updating that date.
